3x3 Word Matrix

How to play

Find the three English words hidden in this puzzle. Touch the first letter box, then touch another box to swap the two letters. When a correct word is made in any row, that row turns green and locks. Keep going to find the rest. Only the three hidden words turn green - other real words you make are decoys and stay white.
